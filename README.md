## The Problem

Businesses across Africa increasingly depend on digital information and software, yet critical operations remain fragmented across:

* PDFs and documents
* Spreadsheets
* Email
* WhatsApp
* Disconnected software systems
* Internal databases
* Institutional knowledge held by individuals

As a result:

* Employees spend significant time searching for information.
* Teams repeatedly answer the same questions.
* Operational data is manually moved between systems.
* Business processes depend heavily on human coordination.
* Valuable organizational knowledge is difficult to access.
* Many organizations lack the engineering capacity to build secure, customized AI systems around their own data.

Generic AI tools can generate answers, but they often lack the **organizational context, permissions, workflows, integrations, and operational intelligence** required to become genuinely useful business systems.

## Product Vision

### Haumex AI Core

Haumex AI Core is envisioned as an **intelligence layer that organizations can adapt to their own operations.**

It is designed to connect existing organizational infrastructure with AI capabilities without requiring businesses to replace the software they already use.

### The platform enables organizations to:

1. Connect documents, knowledge bases, databases, and APIs.
2. Retrieve relevant organizational context for AI interactions.
3. Deploy specialized AI assistants for teams and customers.
4. Orchestrate multi-step workflows across existing software.
5. Automate repetitive operational processes.

7. Build AI-native applications around existing business infrastructure.
